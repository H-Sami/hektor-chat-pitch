# The idea

<div style="margin-top:1rem;font-size:1.15rem;line-height:1.8;max-width:46rem;margin-left:auto;margin-right:auto">

A chat window on <span style="color:#10b981;font-weight:600">/kontakta-oss</span>.

The <span style="color:#10b981;font-weight:600">Hektor Agent</span> answers right away from <span style="color:#10b981;font-weight:600">Hektor's own material</span>: the website, the FAQ, the terms and your service catalogue. When the customer is identified it can also read <span style="color:#10b981;font-weight:600">their record in your CRM</span>.

If it is unsure, or the customer asks for a person, <span style="color:#10b981;font-weight:600">a human takes over in the same window</span>, with the whole conversation already in front of them.

</div>

<!--
Three things in one: instant answers, answers grounded in Hektor's facts, and a human one click away. The form, the phone line and the email address all stay exactly as they are. This is another way in, not a replacement.
-->

# How the Hektor Agent is powered

<div style="margin-top:.5rem;font-size:.86rem;opacity:.85">Writing the answers takes a language model, in the same way a website takes a web host. You choose who supplies it, and you hold the account.</div>

<div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr;gap:1.8rem;font-size:.85rem;margin-top:.9rem">

<div>

**Your choice, your account**

- You open the account: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral, or a supplier inside the EU
- Hektor pays that supplier directly, at their list price. We add nothing on top
- The access key sits with you, and you can replace or cancel it whenever you want
- If you are not happy with the answers, we move to another supplier without touching anything else

</div>

<div>

**What we set up and look after**

- the Hektor Agent, the material it reads, and the rules it follows
- day-to-day running, plus a monthly spending ceiling you set
- a second supplier on standby, so one supplier's bad day does not close your chat
- the quality checks, so any change is measured before it goes live

</div>

</div>

<div style="margin-top:1.3rem;padding:.8rem 1.1rem;border-left:4px solid #10b981;background:rgba(16,185,129,.08);font-size:.88rem">
You are not tied to anyone. The supplier can be changed, and the chat can be switched off, without rebuilding the rest.
</div>

# Data protection and identity

<div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr;gap:1.8rem;font-size:.84rem;margin-top:.4rem">

<div>

**Built in from the start**

- Hektor remains responsible for the data. The supplier works under a written agreement
- we record why each piece of data is used, and the privacy policy is updated for chat
- the Hektor Agent sees only the customer it is talking to, and nothing else
- personal details the answer does not need are hidden before the question is sent on

</div>

<div>

**Controlled, and provable afterwards**

- we confirm who the customer is before showing anything about their account
- conversations and decisions are kept, so a dispute can be settled
- how long records are kept, and when they are deleted, is Hektor's decision
- sales conversations are treated as marketing, service conversations as support

</div>

</div>

<div style="margin-top:1.1rem;padding:.8rem 1.1rem;border-left:4px solid #10b981;background:rgba(16,185,129,.08);font-size:.88rem">
Identity is the gate. No customer number or verified phone number means no customer records, whatever the person in the chat claims.
</div>

<!--

-->
